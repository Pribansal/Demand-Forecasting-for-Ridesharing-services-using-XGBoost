Balancing the supply of drivers with the fluctuating demand for passengers over time and space is a major operational challenge for ride-hailing services like Uber. 
Insufficient driver availability results in longer wait times and lost revenue, while an excess of drivers leads to inefficiencies and lower driver earnings. 
By employing machine learning techniques to analyze and predict ride request patterns in urban environments, this project tackles this challenge through data-driven demand forecasting. 

Project Objectives 
1. Develop accurate models to predict hourly Uber ride demand in New York City 
2. Compare how well different machine learning techniques perform. 
3. Create useful six-month projections to inform driver allocation plans. 
4. Establish a repeatable process for predicting demand over time. 

Effective demand forecasting enables: 
1. Proactive driver positioning in high-demand areas 
2. Dynamic incentive programs to balance supply-demand gaps 
3. Improved customer experience through reduced wait times 
4. More efficient resource utilization and higher driver utilization rates
